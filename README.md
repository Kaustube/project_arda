# ARDA — Colour, Cultivated®

A zero-waste circular economy project by Enactus Bennett University that converts organic food waste into edible mushrooms and food-grade natural dyes.

## What it is

Fruit and vegetable peels collected from campus are sterilized and split into two streams. The solid residue becomes substrate for oyster mushroom cultivation. The pigment-rich liquid becomes chemical-free natural food dye.

One input. Two outputs. Nothing discarded.

## Projects

**Mushroom Cultivation** — Oyster mushrooms grown on sterilized food waste substrate. Peels are boiled, mixed with straw and rice flour, inoculated with spawn, and cultivated in a controlled humid environment.

**Natural Food Dyes** — Pigment-rich liquid extracted during sterilization is filtered and fixed with alum, vinegar, or salt. Sources include beetroot, onion skin, turmeric, and tea leaves.
